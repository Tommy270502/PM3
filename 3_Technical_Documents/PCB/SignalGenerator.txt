The VU meter and Ambient Light uses audio signals as input.
In the end music from a smartphone will be the most likely source.
For testing and debugging, however, music is not very suitable because amplitude and frequency change.
Far better would be a sine wave with selectable amplitude and frequency.
This can be generated by a signal generator.
It is also possible to use the smartphone app phyphox with its "Tongenerator" in the "Akustik" section.
